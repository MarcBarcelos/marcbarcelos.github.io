I'm a PhD Fellow at TEXT: Center for Contemporary Cultures of Text at Aarhus University, where I study **writer identity in human–AI collaboration**, namely what happens to an individual's measurable style when they write with large language models like ChatGPT and Claude. My dissertation develops the **expressive profile pipeline**, which condenses dozens of stylistic features into a handful of core dimensions that capture what makes a writer recognizable. I use it to **compare human and AI-generated text**, measure how co-writing with AI shifts a writer's style, and map where authorship, style, and reception come apart in this co-writing context.

Before my PhD, I earned an M.Sc. in Cognitive Science from Aarhus University and a B.A. in Psychology (magna cum laude) from California State University, Long Beach, and worked as a data analyst at the Aarhus Institute of Advanced Studies. I bring that background in cognitive modeling, statistics, and natural language processing to questions from literary studies.
